

# Story points of interest 
    > Introduction of the mystery (could be artifact or abandoned ship or anomaly)
    > Initial incident, though seemingly minor 
    > Clues discovered, revealing more information about the mystery
    > Problem or threat becomes apparent, stakes are established
    > ....
    > Communication link lost - stakes are raised
    > ....
    > unhinged behavior or mental breakdown or crew 
    > ....
    > Evacuation or self-destruction


    - need multiple decision points for the player to influence the story - maybe 3 or 4 
        - decision points around `Moral dilemma` might be good - force the player to make tough choices

    - points of character development
        - how characters react to the mystery and escalating stakes
        - how characters handle moral dilemmas and tough choices
        - how characters cope with unhinged behavior or mental breakdowns

    - points where more of the mystery is revealed

    - points where the stakes are raised
        - conflict between characters or with external forces
        - unexpected consequences of previous decisions
        - escalation of the central threat or mystery

    - Really define the why for each part of the story, even just for yourself as the writer

    - Paranoia


# Quality Lens

Criterion	Question
Premise	Is the central idea interesting?
Clarity	Can the reader understand the situation?
Character	Is it clear who the story is about?
Objective	Is there a clear goal?
Conflict	Is there a meaningful obstacle?
Stakes	Does the reader understand what could be lost?
Escalation	Does the situation become more complicated?
Resolution	Is there a satisfying outcome?
Originality	Does the story offer something distinctive?
Conciseness	Can unnecessary detail be removed?
